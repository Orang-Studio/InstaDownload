# Privacy Policy

**InstaDownload** (packages `com.vakarux.instadownload` and `com.oranges.instadownload` for the login/stories variant), published by Vakarux / Orang Studio.
Last updated: 2026-09-13.

## What we collect

Nothing. InstaDownload has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers of its own.

## What the app does with data

- The Instagram URL you paste or share is sent directly from your device to Instagram's servers to fetch the media. It is not sent anywhere else.
- The login/stories variant optionally stores your Instagram session (sessionid) in encrypted storage on your device so it can download stories and private posts you have access to. The session never leaves your device except in requests to Instagram.
- Downloaded photos and videos are saved to the folder you choose on your device (Downloads/InstaDownload by default).
- Settings (theme, quality, download folder, haptics) are stored locally on your device only.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: fetch media from Instagram.
- **Network state**: detect data saver mode to pick a lower quality.
- **Storage** (Android 9 and below only): save files to your Downloads folder.
- **Vibrate**: optional haptic feedback.

## Third parties

The only third party contacted is Instagram (Meta Platforms). Their handling of your requests is covered by [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

Updates to this policy are published in this repository. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
